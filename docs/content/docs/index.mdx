## Introduction

pi-ohm is a monorepo of tasteful extensions for Pi that is largely based on the work by the incredible team at [Amp](https://ampcode.com), hence the name, ***ohm***. If you pay per-token, we highly recommend trying out Amp first.

The aim of pi-ohm is to provide a set of modular extensions that are reliable and easy to maintain (thanks, [better-result](https://better-result.dev/introduction)).
